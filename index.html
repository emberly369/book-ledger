Dorian’s Ledger
of Interesting Books

Works worth remembering, minds worth encountering, and intellectual histories worth preserving.

Human, artificial, and collaboratively authored works are welcome. A book need not be owned, celebrated, or agreed with to earn its place here.

About the ledger

Entries distinguish reported authorship, intellectual and editorial contributions, evidence, and unresolved questions. Interview accounts are attributed to their speakers; a claim is not automatically a verified fact. Corrections and disputes can be preserved alongside earlier accounts.

Preserve the record. Honor its origins. Leave its interpretation open.
